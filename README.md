# ⚾ **The Price of Winning: An Analysis of Payroll, Performance, and Efficiency in Major League Baseball**

# 📘 **Project Overview**
This project looks at how payroll spending relates to team performance and efficiency in Major League Baseball from **2000 to 2025**.  
It was inspired by the idea behind *Moneyball*: that smart decisions and data-driven strategies can sometimes beat financial power. I wanted to see if that still holds true today, or if the game has changed.

Using Power BI, Python, and Excel, I explored how payrolls have grown over time, how spending connects to winning, and which teams get the most out of their budgets. The goal was to find out what really drives success in modern baseball, whether it comes from money, management, or something in between.

---

# ❓ **Key Questions**
- How has league spending changed over time?  
- Does a higher payroll actually lead to more wins?  
- Which teams use their payroll most efficiently?  
- How might spending affect fan engagement and attendance? 

---

# 🧰 **Tools and Technologies**
- **Power BI** for modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard design  
- **Python (pandas)** for early data exploration and validation  
- **Excel** for data cleaning and preparation  

---

# 📊 **Data Sources**
- **Steve the Ump** (compiled from USA Today): Historical MLB payroll data (2000–2025)  
- **Baseball Reference:** Team wins, standings, and attendance data (2015–2025)  

---

# 💡 **Key Insights**
- **Payrolls Have Risen Dramatically:** League spending has grown sharply since 2000, widening the gap between large- and small-market teams.  
- **Money Helps, But It’s Not Everything:** Higher payrolls usually lead to more wins, but efficiency varies widely from team to team.  
- **Small Markets Compete Too:** Organizations like Tampa Bay and Cleveland consistently outperform expectations through smart management and player development.  
- **High Spending Doesn’t Guarantee Success:** Several of the top spenders underperformed despite record-breaking payrolls.  

---

# 🔍 **Gaps and Future Analysis**
- Add **market size data** to better explain payroll differences and efficiency  
- Analyze **attendance and fan engagement** as potential outcomes of spending  
- Extend the analysis to include **postseason performance** and return on investment  
- Experiment with **rolling averages** to smooth yearly volatility  

---

# 🏁 **Conclusion**
Spending plays a clear role in winning, but it isn’t the whole story.  
Teams that spend wisely often rival or outperform those with much larger budgets. Success in baseball comes from balancing financial investment with smart decision-making and long-term strategy.
